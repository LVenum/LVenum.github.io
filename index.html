About me

I am a second-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at The University of Texas at Dallas, co-advised by Prof. Feng Chen and Prof. Bingzhe Li. I work on computer-use agents (CUAs) for long-horizon tasks, along two lines: reliability (error detection and recovery, trustworthiness) and persona-aware CUAs, in collaboration with MatrAIx. The second line led me to persona AI and to agentic memory, which I expect persona agents to depend on.

My research background spans LLM-based planning, structured reasoning for scientific writing, and dynamic text-attributed graphs. I was previously advised by Dr. Jessica Ouyang at UT Dallas. I have also worked with Sheng Li at the University of Virginia and Wei Xu at Renmin University of China.

I received my M.S. in Computer Science from Emory University, where I was advised by Prof. Fei Liu, and bachelor's degrees in Data Science and Finance from Renmin University of China.

UNDER REVIEW · ACL ARR OCTOBER 2026

PersonaCUABench: Benchmarking Computer-Use Agents Serving Diverse Personas

Haoming Li†, Defu Cao†, Xu Hu†, Yingtao Luo, Yiyang Wang, Xianghe Xu, Jiashu He, Wanting Jiang, Yifan Zhang, Yunhan Zhao, Siyang Wu, Chen Yang, Haixin Wang, Zheyuan Deng, Taoran Li, et al.

† Equal contribution

A benchmark of 44 personas across 11 domains that holds each task fixed and varies only who asks. Across nine CUA backbones, persona-phrased requests cut mean task completion from 0.689 to 0.441, and even fully completed requests satisfy users less often than the original requests.

UNDER REVIEW · ACL ARR OCTOBER 2026

SAFE-Mem: Secure Agentic Memory with Fine-Grained Authority and Governance-Aware Retrieval

Haoming Li, Dingyi Kang, Xu Hu, Jinxin Yang, Zhu Xu, Chenru Wang, Yi Ge, Miao Yin, Guanpeng Li, Feng Chen, Bingzhe Li

An agentic memory that accounts for both the stored content and its information source. It has the lowest attack success among defenses that keep legitimate memory and the highest answer accuracy of all evaluated defenses.
